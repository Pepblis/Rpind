Meet Our Leaders

Men and women who risk everything for peace in the creeks.

Our President

TM

Comrade / Amb. Toluwa Mulade

President, RPIND

Leading voice for peace in the Niger Delta. He has mediated high-profile kidnappings, engaged the Federal Government on pipeline surveillance, and consistently advocated for inclusion, jobs and development for riverine communities.

Known For: Championing Amnesty, Rescuing Turkish Sailors, Stopping Pipeline Attacks, Fighting for Host Community Rights.

His Message: "Peace is not weakness. Peace is strength. We choose dialogue, because war has cost us too much."

What Makes Our Leadership Different?

✅ We Go To The Creeks

We don't lead from Abuja offices. We sleep in the communities. We eat with the people.

✅ We Are Trusted By All Sides

Militants trust us. Government trusts us. Oil companies trust us. That is why mediation works.

✅ We Stay After The Cameras Leave

Peace is not a photo-op. We follow up, monitor agreements and ensure promises are kept.
