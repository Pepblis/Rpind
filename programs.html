Our Programs & Projects

Peace is not just a word. It is work. These are the programs we run every day in the creeks.

Ongoing Programs

ONGOING • ALL STATES

🕊️ 1. Creek Peace Dialogue Tour

We visit riverine communities one by one — meeting youths, elders, women and ex-militants. We listen to grievances, mediate disputes and build local peace committees.

States: Delta, Bayelsa, Rivers, Edo, Ondo
Goal: Zero pipeline attacks in 2026

ONGOING • YOUTH FOCUSED

👦 2. Youths For Peace, Not Guns

Mentorship, skills training and peace education for youths at risk of militancy. We give them a reason to choose peace — a skill, a job, a future.

Includes: Leadership training, Vocational skills, Sports for Peace

ADVOCACY • NATIONAL

🛢️ 3. Inclusive Surveillance Campaign

National advocacy to ensure pipeline surveillance contracts are inclusive — not for a few elites, but for all who kept the peace: ex-militants, peace groups, host communities.

Target: Presidency, NSA, NNPC, NASS

REHABILITATION

🔄 4. Reintegration & Amnesty Support

We support the Presidential Amnesty Programme by identifying genuine beneficiaries, helping them reintegrate, and following up to ensure they don't return to the creeks.

Focus: Training, Jobs, Mental health support
