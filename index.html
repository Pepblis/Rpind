Who We Are

Riverine Peace Initiative in Niger Delta (RPIND) is a peace-building organization working across the riverine communities of the Niger Delta. We stand between conflict and peace — engaging militants, community leaders, oil companies and government to prevent violence and protect lives.

🕊️ Our Vision

A peaceful, united and developed Niger Delta where riverine communities thrive without fear or marginalization.

🎯 Our Mission

To sustain peace through dialogue, mediation, advocacy, rehabilitation and inclusive development.

💚 Why It Matters

When there is peace, there is jobs, clean water, roads and development. When there is war, everybody loses.
