Press Statements

📢 PRESS STATEMENT — ON INCLUSIVE SURVEILLANCE

We, RPIND, reject any attempt to monopolize pipeline surveillance. Peace was built by many hands. Let all who laboured for peace benefit from peace. Inclusion is not charity — it is justice.

— President, Toluwa Mulade

📢 PRESS STATEMENT — ON HOST COMMUNITY RIGHTS

The Niger Delta cannot continue to produce the wealth of Nigeria while its people drink polluted water and sleep without light. We call on government and oil companies to deliver real development to riverine communities.

— RPIND National Secretariat

📢 PRESS STATEMENT — CALL FOR CALM

We call on all youths in the Niger Delta to embrace dialogue. Violence has never built a school, a road or a hospital. Peace has. Choose peace.

— RPIND Leadership
