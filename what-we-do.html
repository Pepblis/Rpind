What We Do

Six ways we turn conflict into peace — and peace into development for the Niger Delta.

Our 6 Pillars of Peace

1

🕊️ Peace Mediation & Dialogue

We sit with aggrieved youths, militants, traditional rulers, oil companies and government. We listen, we mediate, we stop violence before it starts. No guns. Just talk, truth and trust.

Result: Pipeline attacks stopped, kidnappings resolved, communal clashes prevented.

2

🛢️ Pipeline Surveillance Advocacy

We fight for INCLUSIVE surveillance. Peace was built by many — ex-militants, community leaders, peace groups. It should not be given to only a few elites. Everybody who kept the peace must be carried along.

Result: National voice on Vanguard 2026 for inclusion.

3

🔄 Rehabilitation & Reintegration

RPIND championed the Amnesty idea. We help repentant militants drop weapons and pick up skills, jobs and education. A youth with a job will not carry a gun.

Result: Hundreds reintegrated into peaceful livelihoods.

4

🏘️ Host Community Rights

We demand fair employment for Niger Delta youths, clean water, good roads, schools and hospitals. Oil comes from our land — development must reach our people.
